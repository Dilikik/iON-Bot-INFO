# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 28, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **[operator name]** ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the iON Discord Bot (the "Bot").

## 1. Information the Bot processes

The Bot currently provides slash commands, including `/ping`. To respond to a command, Discord sends the Bot interaction data needed to identify and process that command. The Bot uses the interaction to return the result to the user who invoked it. The current implementation does not intentionally save users' Discord messages, user profiles, or interaction data to a database.

The Bot does not enable the privileged message content intent and does not read ordinary server messages.

## 2. Operational logs

While running, the Bot writes startup information to its application logs, including its Discord account identity and the number of servers it is connected to. The current code does not log command contents or users' Discord IDs. The server or hosting provider running the Bot may independently collect technical logs, such as connection or error records, under its own policies.

## 3. How information is used

Information processed by the Bot is used to provide its commands, keep the Bot operational, and diagnose operational problems. We do not currently use the Bot to build user profiles, sell personal information, or send advertising.

## 4. Storage and retention

The current Bot implementation does not maintain a database or intentionally retain command interaction data. Operational logs may be retained by the Bot operator or hosting provider for as long as needed for operation, security, and troubleshooting, subject to the hosting configuration.

Discord processes information under its own privacy policy. Information may also be processed by the provider hosting the Bot. Please review their policies for details about their processing and retention.

## 5. Sharing

We do not currently sell personal information or share Bot-collected interaction data with third parties. Information may be processed by Discord to deliver the service and by the Bot's hosting provider to run the Bot. We may disclose information if required by law or necessary to protect the rights, safety, and security of users or the service.

## 6. Your choices and requests

You can stop using the Bot or ask a server administrator to remove it from a server. Since the current implementation does not maintain a database of user profiles or command history, it may not have user interaction records to retrieve or delete. For privacy questions or requests, contact **[contact email]**.

Your rights may depend on where you live. We will handle applicable privacy requests in accordance with applicable law.

## 7. Children's privacy

The Bot is not intended to bypass Discord's minimum age requirements. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Discord's own age requirements and privacy terms apply to your use of Discord.

## 8. Security

We take reasonable steps appropriate to the Bot's operation to protect information processed by it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy when the Bot or its data practices change. The "Last updated" date above indicates when it was most recently changed. Continued use of the Bot after an update means you acknowledge the revised Policy.
